ABOUT US

Your local starting point for long-distance travel.

M Tariq Travels (Kahror Pacca) is a private, government-approved bus stand. Different buses and transport services arrive here and connect passengers with cities such as Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, Bahawalpur, Faisalabad and Gujranwala.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for passengers in and around Kahror Pacca to find city-to-city transport at convenient times.
